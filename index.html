01 · FEATURED WORK

M.S. THESIS

Metallic Photonic Crystal Waveguide Biosensor for Highly Sensitive, Low-Cost and Rapid Biosensing

A gold-nanorod grating on a TiO₂ waveguide that hybridizes a guided-mode resonance with a localized surface-plasmon mode. The dual resonance makes the device far more sensitive to refractive-index change than a bare waveguide sensor of the same footprint while staying compatible with a simple LED-based readout instead of a benchtop spectrometer.
